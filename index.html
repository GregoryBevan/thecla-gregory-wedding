Our story

It began with a simple hello, and somehow it felt like the start of something that had been waiting for us all along. From the first conversation, we discovered a friendship that grew gently and surely, shaped by laughter, patience, and the comfort of being completely ourselves with each other.

Over time, the ordinary moments became our favorite memories: long walks, quiet evenings, shared dreams, and the deep certainty that we had found not only love, but a home in one another. Every step brought us closer to this beautiful chapter, and every day since has reminded us how lucky we are to be writing it together.

We are so grateful for the life we are building side by side, and for the people who have supported and celebrated us along the way. We cannot wait to begin this next chapter with you by our side.
